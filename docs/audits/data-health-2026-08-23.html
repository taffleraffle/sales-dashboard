Looking good

Solid

Money coming in is clean and current

627 payments from Stripe and Fanbasis, synced through 22 Aug. Zero duplicates, zero negative amounts, zero impossible dates. The raw payment record is trustworthy.

Nothing points at a record that doesn't exist

Checked 12 different links between tables — commissions to people, payments to clients, appointments to closers, leads to setters. Across 3,000+ connections, zero were broken. The plumbing is sound.

Ad data is flowing daily

1,919 ads and 8,664 daily performance rows, last synced 22 Aug. The Meta pipeline is healthy.

Closers' end-of-day numbers match the tracker

Cash collected agrees between the closer reports and the marketing tracker on 204 of 235 days — 87% exact-to-the-dollar. When both get filled in, they're honest.

Looking bad

Broken

Show rate has three different answers

The number the whole quarter is judged on doesn't have one value. Call logs say 32.6%. The marketing tracker says 52.4%. GHL can't answer at all — 1,885 of 1,889 appointments have a blank outcome, and 1,875 still say "confirmed" long after the call happened.

Whichever screen you open decides what you believe. That's the single biggest problem here.

Pick one source, mark outcomes in it every day, delete the other two from the dashboard

Ad spend is recorded in two different currencies

Since 1 May, the tracker's spend figure has been exactly 0.56× the Meta figure — the same ratio, every single day, for 139 days straight. That's a USD/NZD conversion, not a typing error.

We can't tell which ad produced which booking — but the answer is already sitting there

Campaign tracking is blank on 100% of appointments (1,889) and 100% of setter leads (207). Completely empty, so every "which campaign is working" answer today is a guess.

The good news: the Typeform records do carry it, on 89% of 784 responses, already resolved down to the individual ad. Matching on email or phone connects 422 of 536 bookings since May — 79%. The data was captured. It just never got carried across.

Join bookings to the form records, backfill the history, keep it wired going forward

The client list is missing most of our paying customers

225 of 627 payments — 36%, worth $360,309 — aren't linked to a client. Only 5 of those are a matching problem. For the other 220 ($356,518) no client record exists at all.

The client list holds 59 accounts while several hundred people are paying us. Revenue-per-client, lifetime value and commission are all computed off that 59.

Decide where client records should be created, then backfill from the payment history

Commissions only recalculate when someone opens the page

The ledger has no schedule behind it — it recomputes when a person opens the Commission page and not otherwise. Nobody has opened it since 8 Jul, so the numbers there are 47 days old.

Separately, closer end-of-day reports stopped on 14 Aug while setters kept filing through 21 Aug. Nothing alerts when either goes quiet, so a stale screen looks identical to a current one.

Put commissions on a nightly schedule, and stamp every panel with when it last updated

Needs work

The closer roster is out of date

Ben, Daniel and Ahmad are all still marked as active closers, and Ben has no login attached. Since Ahmad took over on 17 Aug, leaderboards and commission splits are spread across three people doing one job.

Deactivate the two who've handed over

Eight clients exist twice

Same person, same GHL contact, two rows — including Mr Electric Medic, DryMedic Charleston and JP Electric. Inflates the client count and risks paying commission twice on one account.

Merge on GHL contact ID, block duplicates at creation

279 calls were "booked after they started"

Booking time lands hours after the call began — a timezone mismatch between two fields. Mostly late-2025 history and improving, but 14 happened this month. It quietly corrupts any speed-to-lead measurement.

Store both timestamps in the same timezone

Thirty calls are logged more than once

27 calls have duplicate entries, one recorded four separate times. No revenue is double-counted today, but call volume and show rate are both counting the same call twice.

Make the calendar event ID unique on the call log

Where I'd start

  1. Agree one show rate. Everything else is noise until the headline metric has a single owner and a single source.
  2. Fix the currency. One decision, and every cost figure on the dashboard becomes real.
  3. Turn campaign tracking on. It only gets fixed going forward, so every week we wait is a week we can't measure later.
